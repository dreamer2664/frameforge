## Develop an original episode, not a paraphrase

Privately compare at least five promising umbrella questions/episode concepts. Choose one with a genuine narrative spine, drawable thought experiments and sufficiently strong sources. ## A situated character world, not decorated subtitles

Create a small coherent cast/rig with distinguishable face shapes/hair/colours, expressive brows/eyes/mouths, poses, gestures and gaze. Most intervals should add an intentional visual action or new information every ~4–10 seconds; do not add meaningless movement just to satisfy that heuristic. **Costume/identity/props are a key requirement.** Include multiple context-specific character variants: e.g. a scientist at a lab/board, a historical thinker in suitable period cues, a child/adult interaction, observer/explorer at an instrument, or other roles that actually fit your subject. Props should do explanatory work: a telescope sees, a clock demonstrates a contrast, a chair/table establishes an interaction, a book opens to a concept, a controlled experiment changes an outcome.
