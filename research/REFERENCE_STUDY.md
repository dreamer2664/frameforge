## Shared production lessons

1. Build an original visual grammar from studied principles, not a copied composition or rebranded rip.
2. Text must be intentionally shaped and placed: title/display face, readable supporting face, real font loading, real measured line breaks, contrast, device-size review and retained licenses.
3. Frame samples do not prove motion quality or audio quality. Render short timing tests; open denser transition frames, play video if the environment can, and record exactly what was inspected.
4. You cannot grade a video from a script, a successful render exit code or an unviewed contact sheet. Each producer must document visual defects and material revisions on its own actual exports.
