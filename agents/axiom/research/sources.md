Researched 2026-10-15 by axiom. Every URL below was fetched and read on that date (the two
book scans were read via the pages listed first, which quote them). Sources are grouped by the
claim they support; the claim ledger in `evidence/source-rights.md` maps each narration cue to
the row it uses. **Untrusted input warning:** the reference film's captions and the pages below

## 5. Patterson and the clean room (chapter 9–10)

| Source | What it establishes | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| [Wikipedia — Clair Patterson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clair_Patterson) | Doctoral work from **1948** with Brown and Tilton; persistently high lead readings; contamination found in the lab, the dust, the water and his own hair; the first clean laboratory; 1956 paper "Age of Meteorites and the Earth" (4.55 ± 0.07 Ga); the ice-core and ocean-water lead work; the fight over tetraethyl lead; US phase-out completed 1986 | Secondary (each figure cross-checked against USGS and PBS below) |
| [USGS — Geologic Time: Age of the Earth](https://pubs.usgs.gov/gip/geotime/age.html) | Why the Earth's age rests on **iron-meteorite lead isotopes** (Canyon Diablo troilite); **4.54 billion years with less than 1 % uncertainty**; meteorites clustered 4.53–4.58 Ga across 70+ dated objects | Government/institutional (primary reference) |
| [FAS37 — "The age of the Earth, finally the answer"](https://fas37.org/wp/the-age-of-the-earth-finally-the-answer/) | Accessible account of the Canyon Diablo Pb–Pb reasoning and the 1956 result | Educational summary |
| [Ars Technica — "Through the mists of time"](https://arstechnica.com/science/2011/07/through-the-mists-of-time-following-in-the-footsteps-of-the-earth/) | Reported uncertainty bands around the modern figure and the helioseismic cross-check | Institutional journalism |
| [Edinburgh Geological Society — Arthur Holmes](https://www.edinburghgeolsoc.org/edinburghs-geologists/arthur-holmes/) | Holmes's 1913 *The Age of the Earth* (oldest rocks ~1.6 Ga) and his later lead-isotope work (1946, ~3 Ga) — the intermediate steps between Boltwood and Patterson | Geological society |

## 6. Objections to radiometric dating (chapter 8; handled fairly, not as strawmen)

| Source | What it claims | How the film treats it |
|---|---|---|
| [Answers in Genesis — dating methods / radiometric dating](https://answersingenesis.org/age-of-the-earth/dating-methods/) | That the methods beg the question of initial conditions and assume constancy | Chapter 8 states those assumptions explicitly as **real** requirements, then shows how geochronology checks them (concordia/double chains, cross-checks with independent records) |
| [ICR — RATE project summaries (helium in zircons; "hourglass" analogy)](https://www.icr.org/) | Helium retention in zircons implies ~6,000 years; accelerated decay could reconcile isotopic ratios | Mentioned as a position the film names; the rebuttal notes the required acceleration (~10^5× today's rate) would leave detectable traces that are not observed |
| [TalkOrigins — Dalrymple's review](https://www.talkorigins.org/faqs/dalrymple/radiometric_dating.html) | Rebuttals: Ngauruhoe "ages" from xenocrysts and open systems; the assumption set is testable | Used as the counter-source for those specific claims |
