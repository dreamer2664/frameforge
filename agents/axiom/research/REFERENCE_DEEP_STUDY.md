## Transferable lessons I acted on

1. **A question is a visual object.** Their best beats hold a question in a bubble or on a
   screen and let the character *wait* next to it (45 s). → My chapter cards and question
   held on screen (`three_clocks_tease`, `puzzle`, `assumptions`).
2. **One prop can carry a whole beat** (crown, red button, "Near Dead Experience" book,
   microscope, chalkboard, jigsaw). → Every chapter of mine has an explanatory object:
   a folio, a genealogy grid, an outcrop, a cooling ball, an hourglass with a crack,
   a clean-room airlock, a mass spectrometer, a lead-migration diagram.
3. **Emptiness and single numerals are legitimate punctuation** (195 s, 710 s). → I use
   `count_back` (a bare rule of years), `date_stamp` (one date on a page), `permutation`
   moments with one number on a card, and the black-frame-free fade before the credit card.
4. **Costume and role cues beat labels.** Costume changes communicate who is speaking far
   faster than a name tag. → My cast carries band cravat + 17th-century wig (Ussher),
   field-geologist coats (Hutton/Playfair/Hall), Victorian beard and frock coat (Kelvin),
   aprons (Boltwood/Patterson).
5. **Their pacing is roughly a new visual idea every 5–15 s**, with hard cuts and almost no
   dissolves; movement is mostly entering/exiting characters, growing diagrams and simple
   camera scale changes rather than animated transitions.
6. **Colour is used semantically and sparingly**: accent colours (their red/teal) mark the
   important noun of a beat; everything else stays in the paper palette.

## Where I deliberately diverge

- The reference is a **ten-item list**; its structure is breadth, not depth, and each question
  gets ~90 s. My film takes **one question and follows four clocks**, which gives a genuine
  narrative spine, rising stakes and a payoff (chapter 9–10) that a list cannot have.
- Their figures are drawn on a flat white field with grey boxes behind. I use **situated rooms
  and landscapes** (study, archive, coast, lecture hall, laboratory, clean room, ice core) so
  the environments themselves date the scenes.
- They burn the chapter question onto the picture; I keep **1–5-word keywords and diagrams**
  and put the full question only on the chapter card, with narration carrying the argument.
- I add a **source/rights ledger and claim map** — the reference's claims are not auditable.

## Reference-side limitations recorded honestly

- No audio inspection was performed, so nothing here should be read as an assessment of the
