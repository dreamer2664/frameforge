## Act-by-Act Observations
1. **Act I (0.0s - 7.0s) — The Atomic Stroke**:
   - The kinetic ember traces smoothly. However, the transition between the cursive trace and the word 'STROKE' (at 3.5s - 4.5s) can be unified more tightly: the trace line should wrap directly into the crossbar of the 'T' and serif spine of 'S' rather than remaining underneath as an independent element.
2. **Act II (7.0s - 15.0s) — Elastic Tension**:
   - The affine shear works well and creates tangible visual strain. The snap into 'WEIGHT' at 11.5s with the expanding acoustic ring is effective.
   - Refinement opportunity: Increase the typography size and introduce chromatic aberration on the shear stress vector arrows for greater cinematic impact.
3. **Act III (15.0s - 23.0s) — Topological Metamorphosis**:
   - FORM -> MOTION SDF transition is fluid and visually legible. The molten corona and floating contour particles add rich production value.
4. **Act IV (23.0s - 31.0s) — The Counter Portal**:
   - Currently, the glyph "O" remains centered while concentric rings expand around it.
   - Craft issue identified: The *actual counter* (the inner hole of the O) should be the camera aperture! The camera should push *through* the center of the O, expanding the inner negative space so it fills the screen, pulling the viewer into the interior tunnel rather than keeping the O stationary in front of the rings.
5. **Act V (31.0s - 42.0s) — Master Convergence**:
   - Imploding particle field at 31.0s - 33.5s works cleanly.
   - KINETIC lockup strikes with authoritative presence. Golden architectural rule and tagline ("MOTION MADE MANIFEST") resolve with high polish.
   - Refinement: Add an optical light sweep / flare across the KINETIC brand lockup as the final rule resolves.
