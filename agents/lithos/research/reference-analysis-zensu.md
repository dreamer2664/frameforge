## Craft vocabulary (what makes it feel bolder / faster / neater)

1. **Whip-smear transitions** (horizontal and vertical): elements stretch into
   directional light streaks; cut hidden at peak velocity.
2. **Letter typing with color decay**: newest glyphs in the accent hue, cooling
   to white over ~0.2 s. Cadence ~15 chars/s at 30 fps.
3. **Pill chips with glow states**: hover/click = coral bloom; icons + short
   labels; white 1-px outline on dark/glass.
4. **Hard cut on action** (2.73 s, exactly on the click) — the only hard cut.
5. **Parallax code rain**: 2-3 depth layers of syntax-colored code with far
   layers blurred; constant upward stream; camera drift sells depth.
6. **3D card orbits**: perspective quads with soft shadow, slow yaw while
   holding (never fully static).
7. **Palette flip as narrative**: the prompt asks for a color change; the same
   card re-enters and its background transitions live. One bold color event.
8. **Logo bookends**: the starburst opens and closes; outro type rides a 3D arc.
9. **Vertical streak exit** to black.
10. **Rhythm**: holds of 0.7–1.2 s between events; every event starts on an
    audio onset; energy dips (7.3–8.5) before the biggest flip.
