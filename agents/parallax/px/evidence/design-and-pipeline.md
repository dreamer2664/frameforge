# KINETIC — "Give language a body" (parallax)

Production identity: **parallax**. On-screen brand: **KINETIC**. The production
identity never appears in the film. This is a new candidate film, in its own
folder; it is not an edit of the delivered r03 and claims no acceptance of it.

## The idea

Language is heavy. You can feel a word before you read it. This film gives the
word KINETIC a body and then lets the body go.

A word is carved into the end wall of a dark stone hall. One raking shaft of
light travels along the stone and finds the letters one by one, each throwing a
long shadow across the wall. Then the stone lets go: the letters hinge out of
the wall, float into the hall as a field of monoliths at different depths, swing
together into a monumental doorway made of type, and the camera accelerates
through that doorway into a warm chamber — where the letters have landed as a
row on a plinth, upright, lit, and the promise is written in light on the
stone.

The reveal is diegetic: the gate *is* the word. The information the film gives
you is the information of the film.

## Structure

| beat | time (s) | space | camera | what the frame must do |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| hall | 0.0–4.2 | deep hall, piers in the near field, slot of light at x = 12.4 m, amber doorway at the left | slow push, 36° | establish scale and the one hard source; hook inside the first second |
| wall | 4.2–12.2 | the carved word on the rear wall (z = −4 m) | continued push, 35–40° | the light finds the letters; the word assembles in stone |
| release | 12.2–19.4 | the hall, now full of floating letters at seven depths | lateral drift, 40–46° | the stone lets go; the nearest letter sweeps the lens |
| gate | 19.4–26.4 | the letters align into a 6.8 m wide letter-gate at z = −3 m | converging push, then a fast crossing at 48° | the word becomes architecture; the camera goes through it |
| monument | 26.4–40.0 | a warm chamber: graded back wall at z = −34 m, side walls at x = ±20 m, two piers, a plinth | slow rightward drift and settle, 44–45° | the word re-forms as an object; the final image holds and breathes |

## Craft notes

* **Depth is built, not filtered.** Every wide shot carries at least three
  planes (piers near, the word or the field in the middle, the lit far wall),
  real occlusion (letters pass behind piers and in front of the haze), and a
  camera that moves in world space, so parallax is a consequence of the
  geometry rather than a post effect.
* **The type is an object.** The seven letters are extruded solids (font
  contours → triangulation → extrusion → bevel) with real thickness (0.5 m),
  cast shadows, and correct advances from the font itself. They hinge out of
  the wall, tumble at different depths, re-align as architecture, and land as
  a row.
* **One material world, two temperatures.** A single stone material
  (procedural, deterministic) carries the hall, the wall, the piers, the bench,
  the plinth and the chamber. The film is cold (0.048–0.075 linear albedo,
  blue-grey fill) until the gate crossing, then warm (amber key, 3000 K
  practicals) in the chamber. No neon, no bloom-as-decoration, no lens flares.
* **Restrained glow.** The only emissive surfaces are the slot of light, the
  amber doorway, the travelling band on the wall, the chamber's graded glow
  and the copy written in light. Bloom is quarter-resolution and thresholded.
* **Motion blur where it matters.** The renderer takes 2 subframes only when
  the camera moves faster than 0.08 m per frame (the gate crossing and the
  letter flight); static passages take 1, so nothing smears.
* **Depth of field** is distance-graded in post (a real defocus weighted by
  depth), strongest on the letter-field passage and released for the final
  image.

## Technical pipeline

Nothing here uses stock footage, stock audio, or a third-party scene
description. Everything is generated by code that lives in this repository.

| stage | tool | notes |
|---|---|---|
| type | `type3d.py` | fontTools outlines → flattened contours → earcut triangulation → extruded solid with bevel; independent validation in `validate_type.py` (fontTools SVGPathPen, 12/12 glyphs PASS) |
| scene | `scene.py` + `storyboard.json` | all timing, materials, lights, camera keys and copy live in the storyboard; the scene builder turns them into per-frame geometry |
| render | `render3d.py` | a purpose-built numpy rasteriser: z-buffer, perspective-correct attributes, back-face culling, one shadow map with 3×3 PCF, fog, ACES tone map |
| post | `post.py` | exposure, depth-graded defocus, quarter-res bloom, vignette, grain |
| sound | `audio.py` | the whole score is synthesised: drone, dry stone taps, hinge scrapes, sub swell, rising cluster, impact, monument chord, a synthetic hall impulse response, and a LUFS normaliser |
| QC | `qc.py` | container/codec/loudness/faststart checks, the ≤0.5 s review grid, dense 0.1–0.2 s samples, a 640×360 phone check, and the encoded-loop gate |

### Fonts

* **Archivo** (variable, weight 700 for the display cut, 450 for the caption
  cut) — SIL Open Font License 1.1, from Google Fonts. The licence text ships
  in `agents/parallax/fonts/OFL-Archivo.txt` (and in
  `agents/parallax/px/fonts/`).
* **Fraunces** and **Inter** are present in the shared font library for other
  producers' convenience; this film does not use them.

### Audio

100 % synthesised for this film: no samples, no loops, no third-party
recordings, no narration. The loop's audio is strictly periodic (the
convolution uses a transform length equal to the loop length), so the seam is
sample-exact before the AAC encode.

### Determinism

Every texture, every random choice and every score element is seeded. The
storyboard holds the film; re-running the pipeline reproduces the same frames.
