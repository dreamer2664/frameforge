## Observation map (loop, 10.240 s)

- Flat concentric ellipse orbit rings on ink; one warm + one cool dot travel the rings; centre holds "Ideas, / in motion." serif with rule; micro KINETIC tag top-left.
- Orbit phase, glow and type all return to start pose by 10.14-10.24 s (frame at 0.000 s and 10.140 s visually identical) — a true periodic loop.
- Rings are flat ellipses (isometric look); dots never occlude or are occluded by the type; no perspective change.

## Grammar worth keeping (brand DNA)

1. Point -> line -> world narrative spine; the warm travelling light is the protagonist.
2. Two-field palette: ink night + warm paper, amber filament accent, cool secondary dot.
3. Fraunces serif display + Inter micro-labels ("NN / CHAPTER" tags, tracked caps wordmark).
4. Draw-on strokes (line appears as it is drawn), word-wipe reveals, one hard palette cut as a structural event.
5. Restraint: few elements per frame, generous negative space, quiet glow.

## Honest limitations of the baseline (confirmed by my own eyes)

- Entirely planar: every element lives on the screen plane; "frame" and "orbit" are drawn 2D shapes, not space.
- No camera: no perspective change, no dolly/arc, therefore no reveal-by-viewpoint; depth is implied only by glow blur.
- No occlusion, no near/mid/far layering, no cast shadow, no material/lighting model.
- Type is flat glyphs; the morphs are 2D mask operations (well executed, but not spatial).
- ~3 s static end hold: composition stops moving rather than resolving in space.
- Loop is a single idea (orbit) with no spatial development.
- Kinetic's own record: no continuous playback watch, no perceptual audio listening; self-review only.

## What this means for parallax

Keep 1-5 (brand), solve the missing dimension: a real camera with lens perspective, extruded type that
occupies and blocks space, occlusion between planes, light with direction and cast shadow, and camera
moves that reveal information (going through/around letterforms). The leap must be spatial authorship,
not more effects.
