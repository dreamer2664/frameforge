The last line is a property of the measurement, not a defect: the film is a
near-black, static-composition piece, so a *whole-frame mean* barely moves even
while the caption track, the plan, the lift indicator, the log rows, the
corridor and the 41 door symbols are all changing. The contact sheets in this
release (`grid_2fps.png`, every 0.5 s over the whole 70 s, and
`key_frames.png`, 44 frames) are the frame-by-frame proof of continuity.

The three beats of the luma curve are the design: the plan scene lifts the
picture (19.0 at 36–40 s, the red doors), the corridor burns it (24.2 at 52 s,
the red creep), the flash spikes it (143.0), and the end card falls back to the
darkest, quietest value in the film.
