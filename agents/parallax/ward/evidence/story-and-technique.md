# LAST SHIFT — WARD 7 · story and technique

A technique replica of the studied TikTok reel (`@survival_rule_51`, video
7697724949091372310, 129.77 s) with an entirely original story, cut to 70.000 s.

Nothing of the reference's narrative, wording, footage, audio or characters is
reused. What is reproduced is the **form**: portrait 9:16, 30 fps, a flat-vector
infographic on near-black, one diegetic graphic per beat, a word-by-word
lower-third caption track in all caps, and a music bed with a red scare beat.

---

## The story (original)

**St. Aldate's Hospital, Ward 7, level 3. 04:12.**

Ines VARGA is the night porter on a decommissioned ward. Three rules are posted
on the wall: lock the east stair at eleven; count the doors, not the beds; if the
lift stops at four, step out.

At 04:12 the lift stops at a floor the building does not have. The shift log
records a round that nobody was rostered for. Every door on the ward opens at
once, and something is already standing in each one. The linen trolleys move on
their own. Something runs.

The reveal: the ward has forty beds and forty-one doors, and the posted board
always had an empty fourth rule. The end card supplies it:

> **FOUR: IF THE DOOR IS OPEN, IT IS NOT FOR YOU.**

## What is deliberately different

* 70.000 s instead of 129.77 s — the reference's pacing is compressed to a
  single minute and ten, and the beats are re-cut for it.
* No voice. The reference narrates; this film is carried by the caption track
  and the diegetic UI text, over an original synthesised bed.
* Own palette (bg `#0d1014`, amber `#ffb020`, red `#e33a2f`, teal `#24c48a`,
  porter orange `#ff7a18`), own type (Archivo Black + IBM Plex Mono), own
  drawing system, own sound design, own story, own characters, own ending.
* The reference is an airport-terminal story; this is a night-shift hospital
  story. Not a character, a location, a line or an image is shared.
